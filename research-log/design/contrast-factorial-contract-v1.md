## Four arms and common recipe

A=narrow/low, B=broad/low, C=narrow/high, D=broad/high. Each sees the SAME400 latent families,40/category, in the same balanced order. Four cases/family, questions n1,n2,n3,c1,s1 (3Noul+Choice+Score),20judgments/family,1600cases/8000newjudgments/400updates per arm. Match old replay IDs by update position. Each arm starts independently from checkpoints/judgment-full-v0.2/final with identical newly initialized AdamW and seed42; no scaling-run weights or optimizer state carried over.

Use unchanged rank8/alpha16 LoRA, frozen BF16 body, FP32 adapters/heads, FLA recurrence and ORIGINAL reference convolution. Adapter/head LR5e-5/2.5e-5, weight decay.01, clip1, deterministicTorch/cuDNN with benchmarkFalse, TF32off and CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG=:4096:8. Max_units12/unit_batch_size12; max1536tokens without truncation. Preserve ALL20new questions and one canonical old question per primitive. Equal1/6 weight for each new/old×Noul/Choice/Score mean, hard BCE/CE only. Reuse audited loss/batching/optimizer helpers without editing frozen sources. No RL, soft targets, Brier training, explicit consistency loss, new model or runtime optimization.

Low language:200 blueprints,20/category, exactly2families each. High language:400blueprints,40/category, exactly1family each. Use a single newly authored/reviewed400-blueprint pool and a category/author-balanced200subset to avoid mixing old-vs-new author quality with the language-count factor. The control is a fresh matched realization of the narrow/low recipe, not the historical step400checkpoint. Blueprints must differ in meaningful linguistic construction; renamed IDs, format-only changes or minor synonym swaps do not count. Preserve author/source traces and report structural similarity/coverage, not a claim of400 statistically independent reasoning mechanisms.

Language may change expression of evidence, never its facts or interpretation. The question/rubric meaning and labels are identical for A/C and B/D. Prefer byte-identical question/rule definitions across language arms and change evidence realization only; if lexical binding needs alias substitution, retain an explicit reversible mapping and normalized equality checks. Entity/action/source/time/mode/outcome/provenance must survive rendering.

Narrow Score rubrics use2or3levels (200families each); broad uses2,3,4,5 (100families each). Family/case counts and shared evidence stay matched. Vary meaningful decision rules, priorities and intermediate boundaries, not merely option count. Broad and narrow are a bundled rubric-diversity intervention; this design does NOT separately identify cardinality vs rule diversity. Every level must receive legitimate positive examples across the corpus; no dummy/unreachable levels or hidden label changes. Original non-Score semantic questions stay matched where possible; any changed judgment meaning belongs explicitly to the rubric factor. Score ordinality must have a declared meaning; never treat Unknown as a numeric midpoint without a supplied rubric that justifies it.

Private raw facts/programs precede labels. Public inputs contain raw evidence and all rule/candidate definitions, never gold, rationales, IDs, feature truth vectors or derivation traces. Across rubric arms, raw evidence is identical for a given language realization. Within a family use two evidence states×two explicit rubric variants, with genuine evidence and rubric contrasts. Relations only cover valid same-type/answer-space endpoints; no forced label flip or invalid invariance.

Categories: claim_vs_completion; permission_vs_execution; unknown_vs_failure; attribution_and_endorsement; entity_binding; action_binding; temporal_scope; reversal_and_current_state; negation_and_quantifiers; ordered_rubrics.

## Pilot evaluation and decision

Headline endpoints are all four complete400-update arms plus the common baseline. Save complete state at100/200/300/400 and cooperative pauses; intermediate checkpoints are recovery artifacts, not alternative best-epoch selection. No opportunistic update extension. Lock endpoint identities and reporting protocol before scoring assessment. Temperatures (one positive global and one per primitive, fixed range.05..100, same balanced NLL objective) fit only calibration, never assessment. Report raw and both fixed calibration variants; do not choose a variant on assessment outcomes. Keep old frozen finaltest/Jev untouched for this pilot.

Primary metric: category-macro both-correct rate over meaningful contrast relations (evidence/rubric/question axes), alongside Score/rubric subgroup and ordinary accuracy. Secondary: complete-case accuracy; per-category/primitive results; Unknown precision/recall; raw/calibrated NLL/Brier; Score mean MAE; wrong≥.90/.95 WITH confidence coverage; old-task retention. Show paired family bootstrap95% intervals (resample complete families within category), effect B−A/C−A/D−C/D−B and interaction (D−C)−(B−A). One training seed is a screen, not seed-variance evidence. No automatic promotion. Clear consistent benefits justify proposing a second-seed confirmation; inconclusive results do not justify automatic scale/RL/model changes.

Report actual candidate units, real/padded tokens, wall time and language/rubric coverage. Equal updates/judgments do not imply equal FLOPs because K and text length differ. Quoted6–10hGPU was an estimate, not a license to drop data; use smoke timing to update it before full launch.

## Operational safeguards

Only root schedules GPU; one model job at a time. New artifacts under data/reports/checkpoints/contrast-factorial-v1. Repo has unborn HEAD and user-owned staging: no commits/staging/reset/worktree changes; use versioned new paths and hashes instead. Runtime .venv/bin/python with PYTHONPATH=.runtime-training:.; CPU tools set CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=''. Preserve all partial artifacts. Complete checkpoint means weights+Adam+CPU/CUDA RNG+next-position+fingerprints+contiguous history; resume exactly, never silently reset. Add a normal cooperative pause at complete-update boundary before long runs; never rely again on a forced I/O error. Strict smoke checks complete family coverage, finite adapter/head gradients, frozen-body hashes and actual saved-checkpoint next-update equivalence≤1e-7. Failed gates need a concrete fix, never a relaxed criterion.

A durable monitor may continue this approved preparation/smoke/four-arm job. It may never restart the old scaling pipeline or launch follow-on experiments. Stop after reporting the four-arm pilot, or user cancellation.

## Concrete manifest fingerprint interface

All paths are repository-relative. Top-level evaluation_freeze_path+evaluation_freeze_sha256 identify the actual freeze file. base_checkpoint+base_checkpoint_sha256 identify the original initialization; tree hash iterates sorted relative POSIX filenames, hashes filename bytes thenNUL then filebytes, without the absolute root prefix. source_fingerprints and data_fingerprint are nonempty path→SHA256 mappings, checked against actual files. Each arm entry has train_file, suite_file, ordered_family_ids, sha256 (trainingJSONL) and suite_sha256. The emitter uses the runner's exported tree_sha256 helper. Hash-only/truthy declarations are insufficient. The manifest itself is fingerprinted in every checkpoint's runtime/schedule contract.
