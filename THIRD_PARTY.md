## Generated by us

The diagnostic and holdout files in `data/`, the semantic-contrast suite and the paired-language test were authored for this project by AI agents working from explicit specifications, then reviewed by separate reviewers. The training language of the contrast corpora, which is not included here, was written by GPT-5.6 Terra and Sol. Labels come from explicit rules and review, never from Jev.
