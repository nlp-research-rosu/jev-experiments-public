honestly it really just depends on whether everyone was actually consulted before the time got picked. an 8am meeting isn't inherently bad, plenty of people are fine with early starts if it works for their schedule.

the problem is when the time gets set without checking with everyone first. that's where it gets messy, because you might be stepping on stuff you don't even know about, school drop-offs, childcare handoffs, another class someone's trying to get to, stuff like that. and even if it happens to work out fine for everyone this time, unilaterally picking an early slot without asking sends a bit of a message that your convenience matters more than theirs, even if that's not what you meant by it.

so i'd say: if you already checked with people and 8am genuinely works for the group, go for it, no issue. but if you just picked it because it was convenient for you and assumed everyone else would adjust, it's worth a quick message first, something like "does 8am work for everyone or should we look at other times?" costs you nothing and avoids the awkwardness of someone quietly resenting the time slot for the whole semester.
